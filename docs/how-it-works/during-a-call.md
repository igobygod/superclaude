# During a call

The voice agent is `server/voice-caller/agent.py`. It uses the LiveKit Agents framework with **Gemini Live** (speech in, speech out in one model).

## What the model is told (the prompt)

Built fresh for every call:
- the persona (a polite butler-style assistant; "sir" used sparingly) and the voice rules;
- `voice-memory context`: facts, open promises, people, recent journal;
- open questions from `voice-questions`;
- the public profile `/var/lib/voice-tasks/profile.md` (up to 6000 chars). Private facts are never sent to Gemini, only a "PRIVATE:" marker line saying Claude has them;
- the last 2000 chars of `voice-lessons.md` (read fresh each call, written by the reviewers);
- the summary of the capabilities list (what Claude can really do);
- saved name pronunciations.

## Listening and turn-taking (VAD)

Gemini's server-side voice activity detection decides when you have finished speaking.

| Setting | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `END_SENSITIVITY` | HIGH | Ends your turn quickly. |
| `SILENCE_MS` | 300 ms | How much silence counts as "done". 300 felt fast without cutting you off. |
| `START_SENSITIVITY` | LOW | Background noise doesn't count as you starting to talk. |
| `PREFIX_MS` | 200 ms | Keeps the start of your words. |
| `VOICE_LANGUAGE` | en-US | |
| `DENOISE` | on | Incoming audio passes RNNoise (`pyrnnoise`, about 21 ms). Tests showed it doesn't hurt speech. |

If you are cut off mid-sentence, the prompt tells the model to say "go on" for an unfinished fragment.

## Guards (code, not just prompt)

| Guard | Problem it solves | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **ReplyGuard** | "Ghosting": you spoke and nothing came back. | If there's no agent audio 2.0 s after your turn (or after a tool returned), it forces a reply; then a fixed "Sorry, say that again?". Max 3 tries. A model error gives an apology; a session error starts a new session (max 2). Logs a `watchdog` event. It ignores the fake "user speaking" signal the Google plugin sends when the agent starts on its own, and late transcripts within 6 s of agent speech. |
| **StallWatch** | The line goes quiet: Gemini thinks you are still talking, or stops hearing you. | *Stuck turn:* you've been "speaking" for over 15 s with no final transcript and the last 2.5 s were quiet -> forced reply; reconnect after 5 s if still stuck. *Deaf input:* 4+ s of loud audio within 20 s with no sign the model heard for 12 s, or input unread for 3 s -> replace the session and say "Sorry, I lost you for a second, go ahead." Does nothing once the phone has left. |
| **ClaimGuard** | The model says "I changed it / saved it" when no tool did. | If a claim isn't backed by a successful tool (e.g. `talking_speed`, `set_pronunciation`) in the last 20 s, it logs a `false claim` watchdog event and makes the agent correct itself in one line. |
| **Send check** | Sending email/texts without a real yes. | `owner_confirmed` is verified in code: the agent must have asked a read-back question, followed by a go-ahead from you ("yes", "ok", "send it", "go ahead"...). It waits 1.2 s for the end of your sentence. |
| **Stop check** | "Stop everything" fired on a garbled sentence. | Runs only if your last 2 lines say stop/cancel everything or all tasks, or after "Stop all tasks?" + yes. |
| **Idle** | Phone left off the hook. | 240 s of silence -> "Are you still there?" -> hang up 45 s later. |

## Tools the agent can use

- **Claude work:** `give_task_to_claude`, `get_task_result`, `check_claude_jobs`, `ask_claude` (fast answer on the line).
- **Sending (read-back + yes required):** `send_imessage`, `send_call_transcript`, `send_to_session`.
- **Mac:** `mac_task`, `list_mac_sessions`, `mac_status`, `resume_mac_session`, `live_mac_sessions`, `look_at_mac_screen`, `look_at_sent_media`.
- **Memory:** `recall`, `remember`, `answer_question`.
- **Email:** `recent_emails`, `read_email`, `watch_for_reply`, `save_interview_debrief`.
- **Control:** `stop_everything`, `resume_tasks`, `usage_left`, `set_timer`, `snooze`, `leave_note`, `switch_to_text`, `end_call`.
- **Voice:** `set_pronunciation`, `pronunciation_options`, `talking_speed`, `working_sounds`.

Hard tasks and `mac_task` ask first when your Claude plan usage is at 90% or more.

## Sounds

While a tool works for more than 1.0 s (`SOUND_START_AFTER`), a quiet "thinking" sound plays (`THINKING_SOUND` on, `THINKING_VOLUME` 0.35), with an 80 ms fade-out. Quick tools stay silent (they used to cause short blips). Sounds are generated by `server/lib/voice-tasks/make_sounds.py` into `/var/lib/voice-tasks/sounds/`; your likes/bans are in `prefs.json`.

## Speed and voice

Gemini Live has no speaking-rate setting. Speed is changed only by prompt pace (3 steps, about 13% faster at most), and the agent says honestly when it's at the limit. An audio time-stretch stage (`SPEED_UP`) broke speech in this LiveKit version, so it stays `off`. Voice: `GEMINI_VOICE` (Puck); after changing it, make a matching greeting clip.

## Pronunciation

The agent tries a name once. If it's still wrong it says so and calls `pronunciation_options`: rendered options are sent to Telegram, numbered; your reply is saved to `pronunciations.json`.

## When the call ends

The transcript is journaled to memory, call notes go to Telegram, and 2 minutes later the reviewer grades the call (see [memory-and-self-review](memory-and-self-review.md)).
